
🏗️ The "Service-First" Creator Blueprint
1. The Hero: The "Ghosting" Reality Check
Visual: A dark-mode, minimalist graphic of a "Sent" message with no reply, paired with a "0.00" balance notification.
Headline: Stop Getting Ghosted After the Work is Done.
Sub-headline: You spent 5 hours editing that video, only for the brand to disappear. We hold the brand's payment in Escrow before you even press 'Record'.
Primary CTA: Secure Your Next Deal
2. The "DM Loop" Headache (The Friction)
Headline: Death by 1,000 "How much?" DMs.
The Content: Show the annoying back-and-forth every influencer hates:
“What’s your rate for one IG Story?”
“Can we get a discount for 3 posts?”
“Send your media kit...”
“Wait, let me ask my manager first.”
The Solution: One link for your Bio. Brands see your fixed rates, check your availability, and pay upfront. No more chatting for 3 days just to get a "No."
3. The "Service Escrow" Flow (The Trust Loop)
Use a clean, vertical stepper with high-contrast icons.
Booked: Brand selects a service (e.g., "1x TikTok Video") and pays 100% into Escrow.
Notified: You get a notification: "₦50,000 Secured. Start Creating."
Uploaded: You submit the draft/link through the platform.
Paid: Once approved, the funds hit your bank instantly. No chasing. No begging.
4. The "High-End" Portfolio Preview
Visual: A sleek, Apple-style mobile profile that lists services like products.
Shoutout (24hr): ₦20,000
Product Review: ₦50,000
Event Appearance: ₦120,000
Copy: "Your influence is a professional service. Host it on a professional platform."
5. The "10% Commission" Transparency
Headline: We only get paid when you get paid.
The Flex: "We take a flat 10%. In exchange, we handle the contracts, the escrow, the payment processing, and the dispute resolution. You just create."
📱 Mobile UX Design Notes (The "Vibe" Specs)
The "Hustle" Counter: A small, live-updating stat: "Total Payments Secured via Escrow this week: ₦12,450,000."
One-Handed Navigation: All "Book Now" or "Sign Up" buttons should be large and positioned at the bottom for easy thumb access.
Dark Mode & Neon: Use a deep black background with neon green accents for "Success/Paid" states to give it a premium, tech-forward "Google Antigravity" feel.
✍️ Final "Anti-Hustle" Hook
Copy: "Stop being a customer service rep. Go back to being a creator."
Button: Launch My Booking Page
